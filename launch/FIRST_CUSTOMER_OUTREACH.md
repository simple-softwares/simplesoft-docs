## First Call Script

Use this order:

1. What business do you run?
2. How many people will use the system?
3. What are you using today?
4. Where does work currently get lost or delayed?
5. Do you need CRM, invoicing, HR, inventory, helpdesk, or service tickets first?
6. Do you have existing data in Excel or another system?
7. Who should have admin access?
8. Do you want it on your server or a local/demo setup first?
9. What would make this worth paying for in the first week?

Close with:

I will suggest the smallest useful setup first. If SimpleSoft is not a fit, I will tell you directly.
